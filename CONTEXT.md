# Church Hub

Shared language for the church information, participation, and development experiences provided through the member hub.

## Leadership Development

**Leadership Development Course**:
An ordered, member-only collection of leadership lessons intended for approved church members.
_Avoid_: Leadership library, video library

**Leadership Lesson**:
A numbered unit within the Leadership Development Course consisting of one instructional video and, when useful, an optional PDF resource.
_Avoid_: Module, resource item

## Prayer Board

**Prayer Request**:
An approved request for prayer shared with church members on the Prayer Board.
_Avoid_: Prayer post, Prayer & Praise Post

**Prayer Request Update**:
A follow-up added to an approved Prayer Request by its author or an administrator.
_Avoid_: Comment, reply, status update

**Prayer Request Follower**:
A member other than the author who has chosen to receive new Prayer Request Updates for a specific Prayer Request.
_Avoid_: Subscriber, watcher

**Archived Prayer Request**:
A Prayer Request removed from the current Prayer Board by its author, by an administrator, or after three months without a new Prayer Request Update, but retained until an administrator permanently deletes it.
_Avoid_: Expired request, deleted request

**Prayer Request Export**:
An administrator-created, compact record of approved current and archived Prayer Requests and their Prayer Request Updates.
_Avoid_: Backup, prayer report
